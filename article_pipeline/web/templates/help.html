Help & getting started

Everything you need to go from a keyword to a published article.

How it works

  1. Find: on the Dashboard, choose how many videos and click Find videos. The app searches your keywords and keeps the best matches (by views, age and length).
  2. Pick: on Find & pick, compare views, likes, like ratio and channel size. Tick the good ones and click Write selected. Dismiss the rest.
  3. Write: each picked video gets a transcript, an article (with 3 title options and claims to check) and image prompts. About a minute per video.
  4. Publish: open the draft, choose a title, check the flagged claims, create the images, then Copy formatted article into your blog and click Mark as published.

First-time setup

  1. Get a free YouTube Data API key and a free Gemini API key (step-by-step instructions are in the README).
  2. Paste them on API keys and click Save and test keys.
  3. In Menu → Settings, replace the example keywords with your niche.
  4. Optional: in Menu → Prompts, describe your writing style.

Tips for better articles

  • Pick videos with a high likes per 1k views: a sign people found them genuinely useful.
  • Your Prompts page controls the voice, structure and what to remove. Try changes with More → Rewrite on a single draft before writing many.
  • Use one project per niche, each with its own keywords and prompts.
  • Always edit the draft and add your own experience. It makes the article better and genuinely yours.

Limits on the free tiers

  • YouTube search: 10,000 units a day. Each keyword costs 100 units per 50 results.
  • Gemini: free limits per model change often (a few requests per minute, a limited number per day, about 8 hours of video a day for transcripts). When one model runs out, the app switches to your backup models; when all do, the rest waits for Continue writing the next day.
  • Limits reset at midnight Pacific time.

Questions

Where is my data stored? How do I back it up?

On your computer only. In the downloaded app: Mac ~/Library/Application Support/Article Writer, Windows %APPDATA%\Article Writer. Back up data/app.db there (quit the app first).

A task failed. What do I do?

Open Menu → Activity to read what happened, then use Retry failed on the Dashboard. Videos without captions or that are private, members-only or age-restricted can't be transcribed.

"All Gemini models have hit their free daily limit"

Nothing is lost. Unfinished videos stay queued; click Continue writing after midnight Pacific time.

How do I update the app?

Download the latest version from Releases, quit the app, and replace it. Your data is kept separately and stays as it is.

Can I publish the articles anywhere?

Yes, on any platform, as long as you follow its rules and the terms of use: make the article your own, check facts, and respect the original creators' rights.
