Privacy

No accounts, analytics or tracking. Your projects, drafts and API keys stay on this computer.

The app only connects to:

  • YouTube Data API to search for videos (with your key)
  • YouTube to read a video's captions, and i.ytimg.com for thumbnails
  • Google Gemini API to transcribe videos and write articles (with your key)

Those requests follow Google's terms and privacy policies. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use requests to improve its products.

Services

  • YouTube Data API v3: video search and statistics
  • Google Gemini API: transcription, articles and image prompts
